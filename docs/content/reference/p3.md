# Univariate factorization

These operations factor univariate polynomials over the rationals, integers, Gaussian rationals, and
prime fields. All work uses the caller's shared budget. Returned factor lists are
sorted by degree, then by coefficients from the leading one down.

## Conventions

Zero and constants have no factors; their scalar is the input constant.
Squarefree blocks are monic and ordered by increasing multiplicity, over
`QQ`, `QQi` and `ModInt`. `factor_finite_field` accepts arbitrary prime-field
inputs and retains their leading scalar and multiplicities. The lower-level
`factor_mod_p` keeps its original contract: a monic squarefree input, with
invalid inputs rejected as `DOMAIN`. Both return factors independently of the
supplied splitting seed.

`factor_gaussian` factors over `QQi` through exact conjugate norms. It returns
monic factors sorted by degree, then coefficients from leading to constant,
comparing real components before imaginary ones.

Integer factorization uses the first suitable prime from the fixed sequence,
SplitMix64 seed 0, iterative Hensel correction digits, an exact conservative
Landau–Mignotte bound, and recombination by exact trial division with subset and lattice-based paths.
Integer factors are primitive with positive leading coefficients. Rational
factors are monic. Recombination remains exponential in the worst case.

The two checkers prove the product equation. They do not certify
irreducibility or squarefreeness; those are separate claims (Z-C1/Z-C2).
